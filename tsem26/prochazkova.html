Skew structures and representable multiactions

Pavla Procházková

Tallinn University of Technology



Abstract: This talk is intended as an exposition of the topics of my PhD research. I want to start by introducing and motivating the notion of a skew monoidal category, showing some examples and constructions that have appeared in the literature.

I will then give a summary of the main results of my first year here. These concern representability theory for multiactions and their morphisms, which turns out to be a useful tool for characterising a variety of structures of interest in the study of skew monoidal categories. (This part will more or less cover what I presented at the ACT.)

Representability theory for multiactions generalises the classical result of Hermida, which establishes a 2-equivalence between monoidal categories and representable multicategories. In 2017, Bourke and Lack defined skew multicategories and proved an analogous result for skew monoidal categories. The observation that a skew multicategory can be equivalently seen as a certain multiaction allows one to connect this result with the theory of representable multiactions. This not only gives a way of rephrasing the result of Bourke and Lack, but more importantly, the theory provides refinements of the general theorem capturing multiple structures that commonly appear as giving rise to examples of skew monoidal categories.
