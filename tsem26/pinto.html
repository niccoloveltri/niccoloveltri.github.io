A coinductive approach to proof search

Luís Pinto

University of Minho



Abstract: We present a coinductive approach to proof search that extends the Curry-Howard correspondence from proofs to entire proof-search spaces. Potentially infinite search processes are represented by coinductive lambda-terms, while finite descriptions are obtained through a lambda-calculus with fixed points. Formal sums capture proof-search branching and allow complete solution spaces to be represented.

Applications include inhabitation problems both for ordinary inhabitants and for solutions (which encompass finite inhabitants while also admitting infinite ones arising from proof search), as well as counting and coherence problems for the simply typed lambda-calculus. We also discuss an extension to polarized intuitionistic logic yielding results for full intuitionistic propositional logic via full embeddings.

This talk is based on joint work with José Espírito Santo (Univ. Minho, Portugal) and Ralph Matthes (IRIT -- Univ. Toulouse, CNRS, France).
